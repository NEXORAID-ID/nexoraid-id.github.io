# Shared cart candidate
The candidate schema is outside the production migration inventory on purpose. The module is not yet exposed through the Worker or portal. Each account has an independently versioned draft. Compare-and-swap prevents stale writes from silently overwriting other devices. A saved revision is the intended shared checkout request key; clearing after checkout requires the exact confirmed receipt signature. Empty drafts retain a new revision to avoid stale resurrection. Next: integrate the authenticated browser route and portal controls, test concurrent devices and checkout recovery on real isolated D1, prepare the sequential migration plus approved encrypted backup/restore and maintenance evidence, then deploy. Never reuse an uncertain pending local checkout key or overwrite a pending draft. No order, receipt, fee or history mutation occurs in this candidate.
